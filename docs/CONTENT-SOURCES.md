## Design references (observed, not copied)

These were observed on tinkerhub.org and /tinkerspace:
- Instrument Serif italic paired with bold display type
- tilted "paper" cards
- marquee ribbons
- sticker-style labels
- pastel highlight colours (light violet `#c7aee8`, pink `#f79cff`, light red `#f0b6a1`, lime `#c3ff16`)
- a "Be kind. Get involved" call to action

The WIT prototype echoes some of these as a family resemblance. It uses its own palette, illustrations and layout, and **no TinkerHub logos, photos or artwork**.
